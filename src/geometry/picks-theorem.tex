Given a simple polygon with vertices on an integer grid:
$$A = I + \frac{B}{2} - 1 \implies I = A - \frac{B}{2} + 1$$
Where $A$ is the area, $I$ is the number of strictly interior lattice points, and $B$ is the number of boundary lattice points.
\begin{itemize}
    \item $2A = \left|\sum_{i=0}^{n-1} (x_i y_{i+1} - x_{i+1} y_i)\right|$ (Shoelace), indices mod $n$; the signed sum is positive iff the vertices are ccw.
    \item $B = \sum \gcd(|x_{i+1} - x_i|, |y_{i+1} - y_i|)$.
    \item In integers: $I = (2A - B + 2) / 2$.
\end{itemize}
